# Record prepared and delivered replies honestly

The host may persist exact reply text with activity chat-post, role assistant, message_type reply and delivery_status prepared before delivery. A prepared post is not a delivered outcome and grants no authority.

When the host actually observes delivery, capture it truthfully. Otherwise use the registered native source at the next interaction to import the saved delivered reply. Recording is always enabled, but no host completion hook or immediate post-delivery execution is assumed. Do not relabel prepared text or reconstruct unsaved outcomes merely to satisfy a completeness claim.

See [recording](../conventions/chronicles_conv.md) and [chat](../conventions/activity-chat_conv.md).
