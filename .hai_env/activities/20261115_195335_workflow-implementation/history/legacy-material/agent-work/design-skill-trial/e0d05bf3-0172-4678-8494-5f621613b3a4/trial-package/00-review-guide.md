# Neighborhood equipment lending proposal

Draft 1 — 15 November 2026 — unapproved trial proposal. Reviewer: volunteer organizer.

The aim is to let neighbors request equipment, collect and return it on Saturdays, and understand their responsibilities. The supplied brief establishes 20 items, two volunteer coordinators, Saturday-only pickups and returns, and no online payments. No other current arrangements have been established.

Recommend a small coordinator-run booking register, item list and handover record. Both volunteers would see the current commitments. All process details below are proposals, unless explicitly attributed to the brief.

This assignment prepares a design before spending or launching. No purchases, real bookings, operational records or external actions have been made. Accepting a design and authorizing implementation are separate.
