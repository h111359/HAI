# Solution specification

Draft 1 is the authoritative proposal. Every operating detail below is recommended, subject to the [open decisions](00-review-guide.md#decisions-for-review). Only the brief constraints are accepted. Unresolved values require review before use; examples do not settle them.

## 1. People and responsibilities

Propose the organizer as policy decision maker: choose terms, name the records owner and resolve disputes. The organizer's actual authority to bind the group or item owners is unknown (D06).

The two coordinators share requests and handovers. Each request names one handling coordinator and next action; the other is backup. The handling coordinator checks eligibility and availability, communicates outcomes and updates records. The backup reads the same records before taking over and records the handoff. Borrowers receive their own confirmation, terms and receipts, not other borrowers' records.

Proposed borrower terms: supply necessary contact/eligibility information, accept the approved borrowing terms before collection, report delay or incidents and return the item/accessories. No extra staff are assumed. Coordinators are not assumed qualified to repair equipment or decide contested liability. D04/D06 must name competent assessors and decision authority.

## 2. Items and eligibility

Recommend labels ITEM-01 through ITEM-20. Each item entry contains name, item owner, accessories, condition, location and status: available, reserved, on loan or unavailable. Record why an item is unavailable. Only a person authorized under D06 may clear condition concerns for lending.

D01 must define neighborhood, age/group borrowing and evidence requirements. Record only information required by that approved policy, the eligibility outcome and checking coordinator. Do not infer an adult-only rule, identity-copy requirement or residence boundary. With no approved rule, a real eligibility outcome cannot be determined.

D03/D06 must establish the real venue, storage, Saturday hours, access arrangements, item-use guidance and condition assessment. No current location or equipment types are known.

## 3. Reservations and changes

Proposed request fields: borrower name/contact, item, requested pickup/return Saturdays, received time and eligibility outcome. The handling coordinator checks the current register and item condition. Confirm only after eligibility, terms and availability are resolved. Send the item/accessories, both Saturdays, venue/hours, contact route, borrowing terms and incident reporting route; record which terms the borrower accepted.

Requests move from pending to confirmed, declined or cancelled. A confirmed reservation becomes collected, cancelled or no-show. A collected item remains on loan until physical return is recorded or an unresolved loss is recorded. The organizer records any resolution of loss; a financial settlement does not mean physical return occurred.

D02 chooses allocation, ties, limits, booking horizon, cancellations and no-show release time. Recommend first eligible request received with a recorded receipt time. Ties await the selected tie rule or organizer decision. Never privately promise overlapping loans. A confirmation still depends on the previous item being returned in usable condition.

Under the D03 recommendation, an extension needs an available interval, a new return Saturday and a recorded confirmation. Cancellation releases an uncollected booking after recording and notifying the borrower; a no-show releases only at the approved time. Preserve the prior entry and reason when changing a booking. Inform affected borrowers of availability changes.

Recommend one shared register and one coordinator editing bookings at a time; the other passes changes to that editor. D05 selects paper or a shared file and actual access. If the register is unavailable, pause new confirmations until commitments can be checked. For an item physically received, issue a temporary dated receipt and reconcile it into the shared record when access returns. A temporary receipt does not authorize new bookings.

## 4. Collection, return and interruptions

All physical collections and returns occur on Saturdays, as required by the brief. D03 chooses loan duration, venue, hours and missed-appointment arrangements. Incident reports or questions may arrive on other days without creating a non-Saturday handover.

At collection, the coordinator checks the booking and approved eligibility/terms, inspects condition and accessories with the borrower, records actual Saturday and possession, and issues a receipt with return arrangements and reporting contact. If the item is missing or condition cannot be assessed, do not record collection; offer cancellation or another available Saturday.

At return, compare accessories and condition with the collection record and borrower. Record actual Saturday, possession and findings; issue an acknowledgment. It confirms receipt/observations, not agreement on liability. Mark an item available only after required condition assessment; concerns follow section 5.

For a missed return, mark overdue, contact the borrower and arrange a later Saturday. Inform the next affected borrower and offer an available alternative, later Saturday or cancellation. Never offer an out-of-Saturday exception. If one coordinator is absent, the backup uses shared records. If neither can attend or the venue closes, inform booked borrowers and arrange a later Saturday. Unattended drop-offs are not assumed.

## 5. Damage, loss and disputes

Recommend reporting incidents promptly through the chosen contact route. Record report date, item/reservation, observed condition/missing parts, borrower's account, possession/location, next action, handling coordinator and organizer decision. Damaged or lost items become unavailable. Affected bookings follow section 4.

The coordinator records facts without deciding blame. Propose the organizer hears the borrower and consults the item owner/competent assessor before recording a reasoned decision. D04 must decide wear versus damage, repair/replacement responsibility and valuation, time limits, deposits/fees and dispute reconsideration. Recommend reconsideration by someone other than the original decision maker where possible; the availability and authority of that person are unknown.

No online payment route is included. Offline fees, deposits, compensation and refunds remain open, not forbidden or authorized by that constraint. Until D04 is decided, no payment demand or automatic charge follows from an incident. Record whether equipment is repaired and cleared, replaced, still missing or retired. Physical return and incident closure are separate: a returned damaged item can have an open case. Preserve unresolved cases under the selected records policy.

## 6. Records and ownership

Proposed records: item list, request/loan register, confirmations/receipts, incident decisions and current approved terms. There is one authoritative service copy. Its actual storage and access method are D05; this trial folder is not the future service record location.

Minimum loan fields: reservation identifier, borrower name/contact, eligibility outcome, item/accessories, requested/confirmed/actual Saturdays, status, collection/return condition, possession, terms accepted and their identity, handling coordinator, next action and incident reference. Incident records add section 5 facts and decisions. Collect only information needed for the approved purpose; borrowers receive only their own records and terms.

Recommend the group as accountable owner, one coordinator as custodian and the other as backup. Item ownership is separate from responsibility for personal information. D05 must confirm the actual accountable body/person, permission to act, storage/account custody, access, retention durations for each record type, correction/deletion requests, backup/recovery and succession. Do not assume indefinite retention or personal-account ownership.

Recommend dated corrections showing who changed what and why. Avoid copying personal information into unrelated volunteer discussions. Handoffs transfer current commitments, pending decisions, incident facts, approved terms and usable access to the service record. On departure, the owner verifies transfer and removes old access. Paper and shared files need different backup/access procedures, to be specified after D05 chooses the medium.
