## Limits and readiness

Two volunteers may not cover every Saturday; demand and capacity remain unknown. Late return can disrupt later reservations. Equipment condition/types, permitted uses, ownership permission, venue, storage, funding and any safety/insurance arrangements remain D06. No expert safety or legal assessment is provided. Charges and liability remain D04; personal information controls and retention depend on D05. These uncertainties prevent the affected launch work being ready, while leaving the proposal reviewable.

Trial checks concern document consistency and inclusion, not real-world outcomes. No independent reviewer was assigned, and no rehearsal or operational validation has been performed.
