## How it works

For a Markdown backlog request, Backlog-manager uses its own procedure; no skill
is required. For customer requests in PDF, it can resolve the optional pdf skill,
check prerequisites and supply authorized sources, outputs, destinations and task
limits. Activity-specific scratch/evidence locations take precedence over the
upstream example/default output directories. Environment adaptation stays in the
role, leaving the external skill unchanged.

The role remains responsible for reviewing extracted meaning, preserving source
evidence, resolving ambiguity, assigning IDs and updating the catalogue. Extraction
never constitutes acceptance. Other roles or a person can use PDF independently
without adopting Backlog-manager.
